## How I Work

I tend to approach technical problems from both ends:

1. What is the system supposed to accomplish for the business?
2. What is actually happening at the protocol, device, service, or workflow level?

That usually leads to simpler architectures, better diagnostics, clearer documentation, and fewer mysteries at 2:00 AM.
